FAQ

Do you hold or route my funds?

No. Payments go straight from your client's wallet to yours. opnaco is a static web page; there is no backend that could touch money or data.

Where is my invoice stored?

In the link itself (compressed into the URL fragment, which browsers never send to servers) and in your browser's local storage for your history.

How does it detect payment?

Every invoice amount gets a tiny unique fingerprint in its last decimals (e.g. 1,500.003417 USDC). The page asks a public RPC node for USDC transfers to your address since the invoice was created and matches that exact amount.

How does Pro work without an account?

Send 29 USDC to the opnaco treasury from any wallet, then paste the transaction hash. The app verifies it on-chain and unlocks Pro in that browser. Keep the hash — it's your license and works on any device.

Which tokens?

Native USDC (Circle) on Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and Ethereum.
